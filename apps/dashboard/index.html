    Method

    • Source: every live entity matching the query below, read with muniment-query over @arkiv-network/sdk from the endpoint above. No Muniment server, API or private data is involved.
    • Trust: only entities whose on-chain $creator is in the publisher list are counted. The namespace attribute alone proves nothing.
    • Coverage: the scan reads up to 10 pages of 200. If it stops early the page says "partial" and every figure is a sample.
    • Expired records drop out of queries, so figures describe what is live at the stated block, not everything ever published.
    • Median: middle value of completed response_days; for an even count, the mean of the two middle values.
    • Waiting time for unanswered requests is days from filing to the block time of the read, shown separately.
